---
name: restyle-my-clip
description: "Gives the newest clip a whole new look, adding what it needs, and shows the result. Use when the user runs /ripclip:restyle-my-clip."
argument-hint: "[the look]"
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Use RipClip to restyle the newest clip: $ARGUMENTS

Find it with `list_clips` and see the names that exist with `edit_catalog` (caption styles, filters, effects, transitions, text styles, sounds). Make the changes with `style_clip` and `add_to_clip` (and `edit_clip` for caption size, music or silences), wait with `job_status`, then show the result with `preview_frame` at one or two moments and give the editor link.
